Our Services

Everything you need to trade on the Malawi Stock Exchange, from account opening to portfolio management.

Equity Trading Accounts

We open and manage Equity Trading and Money Market accounts, linked to a Central Securities Depository (CSD) account at the Reserve Bank of Malawi, so you can hold shares securely in your own name.

Buy & Sell Orders

We execute buy and sell orders on your behalf on the MSE, on a first-come-first-served basis, with each order valid for 14 days and settled on a T+3 basis.

Portfolio Management

Track your holdings across all 16 MSE-listed companies in one place, with real-time valuations based on the latest published prices.

Broker Support

Message your broker directly through the platform with any questions about your orders, account, or the market.
