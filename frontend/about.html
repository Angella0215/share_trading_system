About Stockbrokers Malawi Limited

Stockbrokers Malawi Limited (SML) is a leading stock brokerage firm in Malawi and a subsidiary of the National Bank of Malawi. We facilitate the buying and selling of shares on the Malawi Stock Exchange (MSE) on behalf of our clients, executing trade orders and managing cash and scrip settlements through the Central Securities Depository (CSD) at the Reserve Bank of Malawi.

What we do

As a licensed stockbroker, SML acts as the intermediary between individual and institutional investors and the Malawi Stock Exchange. We handle account opening, execute buy and sell orders, manage client portfolios, and ensure every transaction complies with MSE trading rules and CSD regulations.

Our commitment

We are committed to transparent, compliant, and client-focused service. Every order placed through SML is subject to our published terms and conditions, including our tiered commission structure, T+3 settlement cycle, and 14-day order validity period.
